## 二、5-1 逐条 AI 化痕迹（初稿原句 ↔ 真人参考写法）

### 诊断-01｜开篇：用"关注度上升"替代事实
- **初稿原句**：*"In recent years, immune checkpoint blockade (ICB) has attracted increasing attention and plays a crucial role in the treatment landscape of lung squamous cell carcinoma (LUSC)."*
- **问题归类**：模板堆砌（D-01／D-02）+ 空洞强调
- **真人参考**：*"Immune checkpoint blockade (ICB) has improved the treatment landscape of lung squamous cell carcinoma (LUSC), **but durable clinical benefit is limited by frequent therapeutic resistance**."*　`PMID 42759446`｜Drug Resist Updat｜IF >10
- **差异实质**：真人在**同一句内**完成「已有进展 → 立即转折给局限」；我用了"关注度 + 关键作用"两个零信息短语，把转折推到下一句，**浪费了首句的定位功能**。

### 诊断-02｜背景句给了排行榜式事实，无机制信息
- **初稿原句**：*"Lung cancer remains one of the most common malignant tumors worldwide, and non-small cell lung cancer accounts for the majority of the cases."*
- **问题归类**：空洞背景（无特异性，可搬至任何肺癌论文）
- **真人参考**：
  - *"Ischemic stroke is **a leading cause of** mortality and long-term disability worldwide."*　`PMID 42753319`
  - *"Metabolic syndrome, **driven by obesity**, **poses** a significant global health burden."*　`PMID 42737322`
- **差异实质**：真人的首句背景给**等级判断**（leading cause）或**驱动因素**（driven by），而不是"最常见""占大多数"这类无指向统计。

### 诊断-03｜过渡冗余三连：Moreover / Furthermore / In addition
- **初稿原句**：*"**Moreover,** accumulating evidence indicates ..."* → *"**Furthermore,** recent studies have demonstrated ..."* → *"**In addition,** the molecular mechanisms ..."*
- **问题归类**：机械连接（D-04）+ **本语料实证 `moreover` 与 `in addition` 出现 0 次**
- **真人参考**：*"**To address these limitations,** we developed hetero/nanoarchitectures combining ..."*　`PMID 42759131`｜IF >10（6 个词完成过渡，零连接词）
- **差异实质**：连接词密度 73.3 vs 18.3——我不是"用错连接词"，而是**把逻辑关系外包给了连接词**，而真人把逻辑关系写进了句子的**信息结构**里。

### 诊断-04｜双重 although 从句铺垫，句长 44 词
- **初稿原句**：*"Although single-cell RNA sequencing and spatial transcriptomics technologies have developed rapidly, and although several studies have explored the immune microenvironment of lung cancer, the key stromal mediators of immune evasion in LUSC are still poorly understood."*
- **问题归类**：通篇长难句 + 缺口句不可证伪
- **真人参考**：*"However, the relationship between CNV heterogeneity at the single-cell level and the histomorphological features that are relied upon in a routine pathological diagnosis **remains unclear, limiting its application in clinical prognostic assessments and precision therapy**."*　`PMID 42745263`｜IF >10
- **差异实质**：真人缺口句 = **先锁未知变量，再给未知的代价**；我的缺口句 = **先夸技术发展，再笼统说"仍不清楚"**。后者缺"损失什么"，无法支撑后文的研究设计。

### 诊断-05｜空洞重要性陈述
- **初稿原句**：*"Therefore, a comprehensive and systematic investigation of the microenvironmental mechanisms underlying ICB resistance in LUSC **is of great importance**."*
- **问题归类**：模板套话（D-09）+ 无信息量形容词（comprehensive／systematic）
- **真人参考**：语料 864 句中**无一处**写"is of great importance"；真人把重要性翻译为**具体后果**：`limiting its application in clinical prognostic assessments and precision therapy`（`PMID 42745263`）
- **差异实质**："很重要"是作者立场；"会限制什么"是可被引证的事实。

### 诊断-06｜目的句使用范围放大器
- **初稿原句**：*"we aimed to **comprehensively** explore the **potential** mechanisms by which CAFs regulate immunotherapy resistance in LUSC."*
- **问题归类**：过度绝对化 + 范围放大
- **真人参考**：*"We aimed to define the **microenvironmental** mechanisms underlying ICB resistance in LUSC."*　`PMID 42759446`
- **差异实质**：真人用**一个限定形容词**（microenvironmental）把范围**收窄**；我用两个放大词（comprehensively／potential）把范围**摊开**。范围越宽，可信度越低。

### 诊断-07｜强化词密度 13.4 倍
- **初稿原句**：*"...play a **pivotal** role..."*、*"...**extremely** complex..."*、*"...provide **novel** insights..."*
- **问题归类**：绝对化强化（D-06）
- **真人基线**：全语料 `groundbreaking`／`remarkable`／`unprecedented`／`revolutionary` **0 次**；`novel` 仅 2 次，且均紧邻操作化定义（`PMID 42754334`）
- **差异实质**：强化词密度 26.7 vs 2.0，hedge/booster 0.75 vs 9.71——**我的语气方向与真人是相反的**。

### 诊断-08｜对自身结论零设限
- **初稿原句**：*"...and we further **demonstrated** that tumor-derived KLK5 engages TMEM158, ... thereby establishing an immunosuppressive niche."*（无任何降级表达）
- **问题归类**：思辨不足、结论强度未标注
- **真人参考**：*"These findings **suggest** that CD4 **may** represent a **promising** immune-related **candidate** signature ..., **warranting further functional validation**."*　`PMID 42737434`（一句内四重降级）
- **差异实质**：真人**主动**给结论设上限；本文确有 knockout 功能实验支持因果，故 `demonstrated` 可保留，但**必须**在结论处以 `may serve as a candidate` 收口（见诊断-10）。

### 诊断-09｜句长节奏单一：短句占比仅 6.7%
- **说明**：全稿 15 句，仅 1 句 ≤15 词；4 句 ≥35 词（最长 44 词）。
- **真人参考（短句下判断）**：*"IGFBP3 and APOD were risk factors, whereas LPL was protective."*　`PMID 42754788`（8 词，纯生信）
- **真人参考（极简设限）**：*"The exploratory findings warrants further validation."*　`PMID 42761260`（6 词）
- **差异实质**：真人**长句承载信息清单、短句下达判断**；我的长句长在**从句嵌套**，短句几乎不用——节奏无法变化。

### 诊断-10｜结尾升华
- **初稿原句**：*"**Taken together,** these findings provide **novel insights** into the mechanism of immunotherapy resistance and **open new avenues** for the development of therapeutic strategies, which **has important implications** for improving the prognosis of LUSC patients."*
- **问题归类**：结尾升华（D-05／D-03／D-09）——**一句内叠加 3 个黑名单短语**
- **真人参考**：
  - *"**Collectively,** these findings suggest that PP, particularly at the high dose, **ameliorated HFD-induced obesity and related metabolic disturbances**; these benefits were **accompanied by** ..."*　`PMID 42737322`（`Collectively` 后直接接具体发现）
  - 展望须带具体待办：*"Future studies should prioritize longitudinal assessment, standardized outcome surveillance, external validation ..., **before these measures can be incorporated into** ..."*　`PMID 42750989`
- **差异实质**：真人的收束句给**发现清单 + 准入门槛**；我的收束句给**情绪价值**。

## 三、5-2 对比回答：同一个科学内容，真人的处理思路

| 写作动作 | 真人思路 | 我的初稿 |
|---|---|---|
| 交代研究发现 | 先把研究结论当作**待检验命题**，动词降级（suggest／may represent／candidate） | 当作**已成立事实**，动词升格（demonstrated／establishing） |
| 交代缺口 | 只写**缺少什么 + 缺了会怎样**，两句内完成 | 先用 although 铺垫技术进展，再笼统说"仍不清楚" |
| 组织段落 | 逻辑关系写进**信息结构**（主语即论点） | 逻辑关系外包给**连接词**（Moreover／Furthermore／In addition） |
| 控制节奏 | 信息堆在长句、判断放在短句，长短交错（SD 9.5） | 全篇长句，节奏平（短句仅 6.7%） |
| 收尾 | 给具体待办 + 准入门槛 | 给"新颖见解 + 新途径 + 重要意义" |

**一句话总结**：我的初稿在**"评价这件事有多重要"**，真人在**"说清这件事缺在哪、到哪一步、下一步是什么"**。
